The Game shows ads through Google AdMob. Google may process your advertising ID and similar data to deliver, personalise and measure ads. In the European Economic Area, the UK and other regions where required, Google's consent form (UMP) is shown on launch and you can change your choice at any time. On Android you can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalisation under Settings → Google → Ads. More info: policies.google.com/technologies/ads. Buying "No Ads" removes banner and interstitial ads; optional rewarded ads may remain.

4. Sharing and transfers

We do not sell your data. It is shared only with our service providers: Google (Firebase, Google Play, AdMob). Their servers may be located outside your country (including the US and Europe); transfers rely on applicable law and the providers' contractual safeguards.

5. Retention

Account data is kept until you delete your account. When you delete it, your profile, score, coins and entitlement records are removed from our servers; data held by Google in its own systems (e.g. Play purchase history, ad logs) is governed by Google's policies. Backups and logs are purged within a reasonable period.

6. Deleting your account and data

In the Game go to Settings → Delete account to permanently delete your data. If you cannot access the Game, follow the steps on the Account & Data Deletion page.
